## Company Name

**Geosustara Enviro Services LLP**

## Overview

Geosustara Enviro Services LLP is a sustainability-driven environmental consulting firm committed to
helping organizations achieve regulatory compliance, optimize resource use, and adopt responsible
environmental practices. The firm was founded with a vision to bridge the gap between environmental
responsibility and practical implementation by delivering integrated environmental and geospatial
solutions.

We provide end-to-end services that go beyond reports. Our approach combines technical expertise,
field experience, scientific analysis, and a deep understanding of evolving environmental regulations
to deliver practical, cost-effective, and sustainable solutions. We work closely with government
bodies, industries, commercial establishments, residential communities, research organizations, and
academic institutions to address diverse environmental challenges.

Our expertise spans environmental compliance, pollution control, waste and water resource management,
GIS mapping and spatial analysis, sustainability consulting, and technical support for research and
academic projects. Whether it is obtaining regulatory approvals, preparing thematic maps for
Environmental Impact Assessment (EIA), optimizing environmental systems, or supporting scientific
research, we provide reliable solutions tailored to each client's needs.

At Geosustara, we believe sustainability should be practical, measurable, and impactful. We support
our clients from initial assessment and planning through implementation and continuous monitoring,
transforming environmental challenges into opportunities for responsible growth and innovation.

## Vision

To be a trusted global partner in sustainability-driven environmental consulting, geospatial
solutions, regulatory compliance, and knowledge dissemination across industry and academia.

## Mission

To deliver practical, cost-effective environmental and geospatial solutions through consulting,
implementation, research support, and capacity building, enabling sustainable development and
informed decision-making.

## Major Services

### 1. Environmental Compliance & Consulting

Comprehensive environmental consulting to ensure statutory compliance and regulatory excellence.

- Regulatory approvals and statutory compliance support
- Environmental documentation, reporting, and management plans
- Environmental audits and site compliance assessments
- Environmental monitoring and baseline studies

### 2. GIS & Geospatial Services

Professional mapping and spatial analysis services for environmental planning, research, and
decision-making.

- Thematic map preparation and cartographic outputs
- GIS-based spatial analysis and modelling
- Land Use/Land Cover (LULC) mapping
- Watershed, terrain, and drainage analysis
- Environmental sensitivity and resource mapping
- GIS support for research, and academic projects

### 3. Pollution Control & Environmental Management

Practical solutions for efficient management of air, water, and waste systems.

- Site-specific pollution control solutions
- Operation and maintenance of pollution control systems
- Water quality assessment and treatment support
- Rainwater harvesting planning and guidance
- Swimming pool water quality and maintenance support
- Solid and liquid waste management solutions

### 4. Sustainability & Eco Solutions

Integrated sustainability strategies for environmentally responsible development.

- Sustainability planning for resorts, commercial, and residential projects
- Green building and green certification support
- ESG and sustainability reporting
- Resource efficiency and environmental performance improvement
- Climate-resilient and nature-based solution advisory

### 5. Training, Research & Technical Support

Building knowledge and technical capacity for industry and academia.

- Environmental awareness and capacity-building programs
- Professional training for industry personnel
- Academic collaboration and research consultancy
- Technical guidance for dissertations, projects, and scientific studies
- Data analysis, report preparation, and environmental research support
